For parents and guardians: Merinaz Canvas is a children’s drawing and painting app designed for the App Store Kids Category. We do not collect personal information from children. Artwork and preferences stay on the device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Merinaz Canvas (“the App”), published by Appnextra and owned by Art House Painting Pty Ltd (“we,” “us”), handles information when you or your child use the App on iPhone or iPad. Merinaz Canvas is built as a local, offline-first creative studio. It does not require an account and is not designed to collect personal data.

1. Who this policy is for

Merinaz Canvas is intended for children and families. This policy is written so parents and guardians can understand how the App works under COPPA-oriented expectations for kids’ apps: no personal data collection from children, no behavioral advertising, and no third-party tracking for advertising or analytics profiles.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. In particular, the App does not:

  • Create or require user accounts, usernames, or passwords
  • Ask children for name, email address, phone number, address, or other contact details
  • Collect precise location, contacts, photos from the library (unless the user chooses a system share/export destination), or microphone/camera content for social features
  • Use persistent identifiers to track children across apps or websites
  • Include third-party advertising, analytics, or behavioral targeting SDKs

If a future version ever needed limited data for a parent-facing feature, we would update this policy, revise App Store privacy disclosures, and obtain any required parental consent before collecting personal information from a child.

3. What stays on the device (local storage only)

Creative content and settings are stored locally on the device using on-device storage (for example, the app’s Documents area and preferences). This may include:

  • Drawings and painting data in the local gallery
  • Art Journal entries (finished pieces, notes, and mood tags you choose to save)
  • App preferences and studio settings (tools, paper style, and similar UI choices)
  • Template selections and in-progress canvas state needed to resume work

This information is not uploaded to Merinaz Canvas servers. We do not operate cloud accounts for the App. Local data may be included in the user’s own device backups managed by Apple (for example, iCloud Backup or computer backups) according to the user’s Apple ID and device settings—not because Merinaz Canvas transmits content to our systems.

4. Sharing, export, and system features

The App may offer Share and Export actions (for example, image or PDF) that use Apple’s system share sheet. When a parent, guardian, or user chooses to share or save a creation, content is handled by the destination they select (Photos, Files, Messages, AirDrop, another app, and so on). Merinaz Canvas does not operate an in-app social network, chat, public gallery, or friend system that collects personal information from children.

Any sharing is initiated by the user through iOS/iPadOS system interfaces. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise sharing and to use device Screen Time and Family Sharing controls as they see fit.

5. No advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs

Merinaz Canvas does not include third-party advertising networks, cross-app tracking for advertising, or analytics SDKs that profile children. We do not sell personal information. We do not engage in behavioral advertising targeted at children.

6. Internet and network use

Core drawing, gallery, Art Journal, and template features are designed to work with content stored on the device. The App is not built around cloud sync or online accounts. If the App later uses the network for a limited purpose (for example, loading optional parent-approved content), that use will be disclosed in an updated policy and in App Store Connect privacy labels.

7. Children’s privacy (COPPA-oriented)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). Because the App does not solicit personal information from children and does not include social features that gather such information, there is no child personal data for us to delete from our servers. Parents and guardians can remove locally stored artwork and journal entries by deleting them in the App or by deleting the App from the device, which removes the App’s local data.

8. Parental controls and oversight

Parents and guardians remain in control of the device. We recommend reviewing drawings before they are shared outside the App, using Apple Family Sharing and Screen Time, and keeping App Store purchases under parental approval settings. Merinaz Canvas does not provide a separate parent dashboard that collects child contact information.

9. Data security

Because content remains on the device, security depends largely on the device passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, and Apple’s platform protections. We do not maintain a remote database of children’s drawings or personal profiles. Users should protect their device and Apple ID like any other personal device.

10. Third-party services

Aside from Apple’s operating system and system share destinations the user chooses, Merinaz Canvas does not integrate third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics services. Links to this Privacy Policy or Terms of Use hosted on the web (for example, GitHub Pages) are informational pages and do not collect App usage data from children inside the App.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product changes or legal requirements. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Material changes that affect children’s privacy will be reflected in App Store Connect disclosures as required.
